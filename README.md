GatherLink – Your All-in-One College Event Hub
What is GatherLink?
GatherLink is a powerful and intuitive platform that brings college clubs and students together by providing a centralized space for event discovery, participation, and management. Our mission is to streamline event coordination, boost student engagement, and simplify access to event-related resources.

✨ Key Features
📌 Effortless Event Hosting for College Clubs
College clubs can quickly create, manage, and promote events with ease. Whether it’s a technical workshop, cultural fest, hackathon, or expert talk, GatherLink ensures maximum reach and student participation.

💬 Instant Communication via Live Chat
Stay connected in real time! Our interactive chatbox lets organizers and attendees engage in live discussions, ask questions, receive updates, and interact directly with club representatives.

🎓 One-Click Certificate Downloads
Attended an event? Get your participation or achievement certificate instantly! Our system automatically verifies attendance and generates certificates, eliminating manual distribution.

📅 Comprehensive & User-Friendly Dashboard
For College Clubs: Manage events, track registrations, respond to queries, and distribute certificates—all in one place.
For Students: Discover events, register, engage with organizers, and keep track of past participation effortlessly.
